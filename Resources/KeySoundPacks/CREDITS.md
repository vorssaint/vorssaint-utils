## Provenance: please verify before shipping

These files were copied from the bundle of **Haptyk 1.1.4** (`com.olidev.haptyk.app`, whose
Info.plist states "Copyright © 2026 olvvier. MIT License."). Haptyk had already processed the
original recordings into soft/medium/hard/slam variants at 48 kHz mono. The licenses above are
what each `pack.json` declares. They have not been independently checked against the upstream
repositories, and the full MIT notices (copyright lines) are not included here. Before
distributing, confirm the licenses at the source, add the MIT notice text, or record your own
samples.
