## Where your product appears

Vorssaint brings everyday Mac utilities together in one free, open-source app. Sponsorship helps support its development and introduces your product to people exploring the project.

| Placement | What's included |
| --- | --- |
| **Official website** · [vorssaint.com](https://vorssaint.com) | Your logo, product name and a link in the sponsors section. |
| **GitHub README** · [Vorssaint](README.md#sponsors) | Your logo, product name and a link in the opening section of the README. |

**US$500 per month covers both placements**, for the agreed sponsorship period. Sponsor placements are clearly labeled. We confirm the layout and dates with you before anything goes live.

## Be our first sponsor

The first spot is open. Your product could be the first name people see in our sponsors section.

Email **[hello@vorssaint.com](mailto:hello@vorssaint.com?subject=Vorssaint%20sponsorship)** with:

- Your product name and website.
- A short description of what it does.
- Your preferred start date.

We'll confirm availability and whether the product is a good fit, then agree on the placement, dates and payment method. Once confirmed, send your logo and preferred destination link so we can prepare the listing.

## Payment

Payment will be arranged through **GitHub Sponsors** or **Buy Me a Coffee**, as agreed by email. Please contact us and wait for confirmation before paying for a placement.

## Just want to support the project?

You can also [buy us a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/vorssaint) or support [Vorssaint on GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/vorssaint). General contributions help the project, but do not automatically reserve a product placement.
